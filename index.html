07 · Questions

Plain English,
no fine print.

Is cash really free, or is there a catch? +
Really free. No fee on physical cash sales, ever, on every tier. Cash money in hand, log it in CHEQout, you keep 100%. We make our money on subscription, on digital P2P rails (Venmo, Zelle, PayPal, Cash App), and on card transactions where there's an actual processing cost. Cash gets you on the platform and in our data — that's the trade and we're happy with it.
Why does card cost more than the other rails? +
Because card actually costs more. Stripe charges 2.9% + 30¢ to run a credit card transaction — that's their network fee, not ours. We add our tier fee on top, and we show the customer the total honestly. They can always pick Cash (free), or Venmo/Zelle/PayPal/Cash App (cheaper than card). The customer has real choice. Square hides the card fee from the buyer and charges the vendor instead. We made it visible and gave the customer an out.
How does the gross-up actually work? +
You set a net price — what you want to make. At checkout, we calculate what the customer needs to pay so that after our fee (and Stripe's, on cards), you're left with exactly what you priced. The customer picks a rail and sees one clean number. The (i) tooltip breaks it down if they want to see it. You always net what you set. No deduction from your payout, no surprise reconciliation.
Do I need any hardware? +
No. Your phone is the POS. The buyer's phone is the card reader. They scan a QR, pick how they want to pay, confirm. No $300 terminal to ship. No reader to lose at the booth. The architecture was designed this way from day one.
Can I switch tiers? +
Up or down, any time, no penalty. Stay on the right tier for where your business actually is.
What does CHEQout actually take? +
A subscription ($0–$249/mo) and a percentage on non-cash sales (built into the customer-facing price via gross-up). Cash is free across every tier. We don't charge per-transaction fixed fees, per-location surcharges, or onboarding fees. We don't sell your data.
