A new way to pay.

No reader. No printer. No counter. CHEQout pioneered phone-to-phone payment — the first POS where the customer pays from their own phone. You ring up the sale on your end, your screen displays a QR code, the buyer scans it, and the cart opens in their browser. No app to download. No hardware to share.

They see every option, side by side. Cash. Venmo. Zelle. PayPal. Cash App. Card. Every price, every rail, all at once. They tap the one that fits, pay from their phone, and your phone confirms in realtime — vendor-confirmed for cash, auto-confirmed for digital and card.

No paper, either. Receipts arrive by SMS or email — never on a roll of thermal paper. There's no printer, no register tape, no countertop hardware to keep running. The whole transaction lives on the two phones that ran it.

Two phones. One QR code. No hardware. That's the entire system. The checkout counter got replaced with a screen the customer was already holding.

Set your price. Net your price.

Net what you set. Set the net price on every item. When a customer pays cash or direct transfer, they pay your number. When they choose card, the Stripe processor fee gets added on top — automatically. You always net the price you set.

Same column twice. By design.

Most POS apps treat fees as the vendor's problem. Square, Shopify, Toast — they take 2.9% off the top of every card sale. You set $100, you net $97.10. The fee is your problem.

CHEQout treats fees as the rail's problem. Card is more expensive to run than cash or direct transfer — so the customer who chooses card sees that cost in their total. The customer who pays cash or direct transfer pays less. You always net your price, regardless of how they pay.

Vendor-first, so you can put the customer first. Your prices stay clean. Your margins stay yours. The customer always has cheaper options if they want them.

Plain English, no fine print.

Is cash really free, or is there a catch? +
No catch. Cash is 0% on every tier — Hustle, Shop, Pro, and Empire. When a buyer taps Cash, your phone confirms the handshake, the sale is recorded, and you keep every dollar. We made cash the loss leader on purpose: fewer sellers dodge logging it, so your inventory stays accurate across every channel.
Why does card cost more than the other rails? +
Because card actually costs more to run. Stripe charges 2.9% + 30¢ to process every card transaction — that fee exists whether anyone hides it or not. Most POS apps pull it quietly out of your pocket. We pass it through to the customer paying card, so the customer who chooses a cheaper rail (cash, Venmo, Zelle, PayPal, Cash App) actually pays less. On Hustle, there's a 5% platform fee on top of Stripe; on Shop, Pro, and Empire, it's just Stripe.
How does the gross-up actually work? +
You post a net price — the amount you want to end up with. When a buyer picks card, the Stripe fee gets added to their total. Same item, different rail prices: cash and direct transfer pay the net you posted, card pays the net plus Stripe's cut. You net what you posted, regardless of which rail they pick. The customer who chooses the expensive rail covers the cost of running it.
Do I need any hardware? +
No card reader, no countertop terminal, no docking station. Just your phone with cell signal. Pro and Empire vendors can add a Rollo label printer for one-tap shipping labels on online orders — that's optional, and there's no other hardware in the stack.
Can I switch tiers? +
Anytime. Upgrade when you're ready, downgrade if your season ends. Your storefront URL, your customer database, your inventory, and your Stacks all stay yours across the change. Features turn on and off based on your current tier — your data never gets reset.
How does CHEQout make money? +
On paid tiers, CHEQout takes $0 per sale. Our income comes from the monthly subscription, not your transactions. Square and Shopify charge 2.9% on every card forever — and most of them also charge a monthly subscription on top. We charge a flat monthly fee. Once your sales pass roughly $3,000 a month, you're paying yourself the difference. In-person, your cash and direct-transfer rails don't touch card fees at all. On Hustle (free), we take 5% to cover the basics. Cash is always free, every tier.
What's the CHEQout inbox I get when I sign up? +
Every vendor gets yourhandle@cheqout.store the moment they claim a handle. Read-only inbox in your dashboard — it's where the payment confirmation emails from Venmo, PayPal, Cash App, and Zelle land so CHEQout can auto-close sales for you. Not a general-purpose email account, but your handle is yours to use on hangtags, social bios, anywhere you want.
Do I have to deal with sales tax myself? +
Yes. CHEQout is a POS tool, not a tax authority. But Stacks makes it the easy part: set your state and city sales tax rate to its own Stack, and every sale auto-allocates the right amount. You'll never be short come filing day.
